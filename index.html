What's worth importing?

Bloomerang's CRM price was quoted for under 1,000 records, and organizations count as records too. VHF has 2,005 records in the import file.* Every record is scored 0–100 on how fresh it is, how reachable it is, what the person has given and how much they use the farm today. The page keeps the best records up to the record budget and shows what gets left out. Change any weight or rule on the right; your changes stay in your own browser.

value = freshness × completeness × ( past weight × giving + potential weight × farm use )

CRM accounts to import
How many of this source's records go into Bloomerang now, out of how many it has. This is the selection number. One person can come from several sources, so the column adds up to more than the total.
Value imported
Every record gets a score from 0 to 100: how fresh it is × how reachable it is (email, mail, phone) × what the person gave and how much they use the farm. This is the sum of the scores of the accounts imported from this source. Gold is the part that comes from giving, green the part from farm use.
Value per account
Value imported divided by accounts to import: the average score. Around 50 or more is a recent, reachable donor or regular farm user; under 10 usually means stale or hard to reach.
Value kept
The share of all records' value that the imported accounts carry. Leaving out low scorers costs little value; leaving out high scorers costs a lot.
Touched 2024+ · Contact
Share of the source's records with any activity since Jan 1, 2024, and how many have 0, 1, 2 or 3 of email, mailing address and phone.
Given · Since 2024
What everyone this source brought in has given, lifetime and since Jan 1, 2024, whether or not they make the cut. One gift can show under several sources.

Reading it (default weights)

    How the score works
    • Freshness uses the most recent date anywhere on the record: a gift, a workshop or event, a Regpack registration, the sponsor list, or the live Constant Contact list. Agreed weights: 2026 = 1.00, 2025 = 0.95, 2024 = 0.90, then a drop to 0.50 for 2023, 0.40 for 2022 and 0.30 before that.
    • Completeness counts valid email, mailing address and phone. 3/3 is premium (1.00); 2/3 counts more than 1/3; name-only records keep a small floor.
    • Giving blends total dollars and number of gifts on a log scale, so by default $25K or 24 gifts earns full marks and one $1.2M foundation can't drown out everyone else. Luncheon tickets are not counted as gifts.
    • Farm use blends role (veteran 1.0, volunteer 0.9, family or caregiver 0.8, workshop attendee 0.7, Regpack with no role 0.5, sponsor 0.5, 5K or luncheon 0.4, newsletter 0.2) with how many workshops and events they came to (20 earns full marks). Every one of these weights can be changed on the right.
    • A person who appears in three imports counts toward all three. Giving platforms and pass-through accounts (PayPal Giving Fund, Fidelity Charitable, Anonymous, Race Roster and similar) score zero because the relationship is with the people behind them.
