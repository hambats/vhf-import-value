What's worth importing?

Bloomerang's CRM price was quoted for under 1,000 records, and organizations count as records too. VHF has 1,865 in the import file plus 5,908 newsletter subscribers. Every record is scored 0–100 on how fresh it is, how reachable it is, what the person has given and how much they use the farm today. The page keeps the best records up to the record budget and shows what gets left out. Change any weight or rule on the right; your changes stay in your own browser.

Reading it (default weights)

    How the score works
    • Freshness uses the most recent date anywhere on the record: a gift, a workshop or event, a Regpack registration, the sponsor list, or the live Constant Contact list. Agreed weights: 2026 = 1.00, 2025 = 0.95, 2024 = 0.90, then a drop to 0.50 for 2023, 0.40 for 2022 and 0.30 before that.
    • Completeness counts valid email, mailing address and phone. 3/3 is premium (1.00); 2/3 counts more than 1/3; name-only records keep a small floor.
    • Giving blends total dollars and number of gifts on a log scale, so by default $25K or 24 gifts earns full marks and one $1.2M foundation can't drown out everyone else. Luncheon tickets are not counted as gifts.
    • Farm use blends role (veteran 1.0, volunteer 0.9, family or caregiver 0.8, workshop attendee 0.7, Regpack with no role 0.5, sponsor 0.5, 5K or luncheon 0.4, newsletter 0.2) with how many workshops and events they came to (20 earns full marks). Every one of these weights can be changed on the right.
    • A person who appears in three imports counts toward all three. Giving platforms and pass-through accounts (PayPal Giving Fund, Fidelity Charitable, Anonymous, Race Roster and similar) score zero because the relationship is with the people behind them.
